## Design Context

### Users
Tech recruiters scanning for qualifications, engineering leads evaluating technical
depth, and peers exploring shared interests. The page must communicate competence
at a glance and reward a deeper read with concrete detail.

### Brand Personality
**Minimal, factual, calm.** Information over persuasion. The page should read like
a well-organized résumé: every line is a fact, nothing is decoration.

### Emotional Goals
- **Clarity** — "I understood this person in ten seconds"
- **Trust** — "the claims are specific and verifiable"
- **Restraint** — "nothing here is padding"

### Aesthetic Direction
**Reference:** Marc Lou's product sites (shipfa.st, datafa.st, codefa.st) —
white canvas, one saturated accent, hairline borders, generous whitespace, a
stacked hero (square rounded photo above the name/role/summary), a fact strip,
and simple stacked sections. Experience and Research are rule-separated bullet
lists, not cards. No navbar and no footer; the contact band is full-bleed.

- **White + blue**, single accent, no second hue.
- No glassmorphism, no mesh gradients, no glows, no emoji.

**Anti-patterns to avoid:**
- Marketing headlines, taglines, or philosophical copy
- Numbered micro-labels ("01 / Philosophy"), extreme letter-spacing
- Decorative motion, parallax, or scroll effects
- Dark themes, gradients, glass cards

### Typography
- **Everything:** Plus Jakarta Sans (400–800).
- Headings: bold, `tracking-tight`.
- Section labels: 12px, semibold, uppercase, `tracking-[0.2em]`, blue-600.
- Body: 14px, `leading-relaxed`, slate-600.

### Color System
Light-only, Tailwind defaults:
- **Page:** `#ffffff`
- **Subtle surface:** `slate-50`
- **Border:** `slate-200`
- **Text:** `slate-900` / `slate-600` / `slate-400`
- **Accent:** `blue-600` (`#2563eb`), hover `blue-700`, tint `blue-50`

### Motion
No motion except the annotation layer. The page itself is static — no scroll
reveals, parallax, or hover animation beyond colour/border transitions.

### Annotations (hand-drawn layer)
A restrained "designer marked it up with a pen" layer, built on Rough Notation
through `components/annotations` (`Annotation`, `SketchArrow`).

- **One pen.** Shared defaults in `Annotation.tsx`: `blue-600` ink, stroke 2,
  padding 3, 2 iterations, multiline, ~600ms draw, once per scroll into view.
  `highlight` is a lighter `blue-200` marker swipe behind the text (kept light
  so the copy stays legible on top); use it only on dark text.
- **Budget.** At most ~1 mark per section. Facts and Stack intentionally have
  none.
- **Current marks.** Hero underline on “AI Researcher”; Experience highlights
  on “+19.6%”, “payment routing layer”, “leading a team of 5” and
  “high-growth organization”; Research underline on the DOI; Contact
  hand-drawn arrow → email.
- **Decorative only.** `pointer-events: none`, `aria-hidden`, and under
  `prefers-reduced-motion` they render statically (no draw animation).
- Never required to understand the content, never covering headlines or CTAs.

### Spacing & Layout
- Max width: `64rem` (`max-w-content`), centered.
- Section rhythm: `pt-20`; the contact band is full-bleed edge-to-edge with no
  outer margin and no rounded corners.
- Experience / Research: no cards — a bold heading line per entry, `divide-y`
  hairlines between entries, and bullet lists with a small blue dot marker.
- Stack: icon-only rounded-full pills in the original order.

### Content Principles
1. **Facts only.** Role, company, period, metrics, stack, links — no adjectives.
2. **One idea per bullet.** Every sentence is its own bullet; keep them
   quantitative.
3. **No titles or phrases.** Remove anything that isn't information.
4. **Scannable first.** Bold the role and metric; mute the rest.
